Monopoly Deal Rules

The fast-paced, addictive card game where your luck can change in the play of a card!

👥 2–5 Players ⏱️ ~15 Minutes 🎯 Goal: 3 Full Sets

🏆 The Objective

Be the first player to collect 3 complete Property Sets of different colors in front of you on the table.

🔄 What to Do On Your Turn

1. Draw
Draw 2 Cards

Take 2 cards from the Draw Pile. If you start your turn with 0 cards in your hand, draw 5 cards instead!

2. Play
Play Up to 3 Cards

You may play 0, 1, 2, or up to 3 cards in any combination:

  • Bank Money/Action Cards for value ($M).
  • Lay down Properties in front of you.
  • Play Action Cards to collect rent or steal cards!
3. End Turn
Discard if > 7 Cards

You can never have more than 7 cards in your hand at the end of your turn. Discard extras to the Discard Pile, then click Pass.

🃏 Types of Cards

🏠 Property & Wildcard Cards

Play property cards face-up in front of you. Each color requires a specific set size to be complete:

Brown: 2 cards
Light Blue: 3 cards
Pink: 3 cards
Orange: 3 cards
Red: 3 cards
Yellow: 3 cards
Dark Green: 3 cards
Dark Blue: 2 cards
Railroads (Black): 4 cards
Utilities (Light Green): 2 cards

Wildcard Properties: Can be flipped or switched between colors to help complete sets.

💰 Money & Bank

Play Money cards directly into your Bank. Money in your bank is safe from most actions and is used to pay rent and debts.

Banking Action Cards: You can also put Action cards into your Bank as money using their dollar value (top corner). Once banked, they can only be used as cash, not for their action!

⚡ Action Cards Explained

Card Bank Value Effect
Deal Breaker 5M Steal a complete property set from another player, including any buildings on it!
Just Say No 4M Cancel any action card played against you (rent, steal, debt). Can also be countered with another Just Say No!
Sly Deal 3M Steal a single property card from an opponent (cannot steal from a completed set).
Forced Deal 3M Swap one of your properties with an opponent's property (cannot swap from completed sets).
Debt Collector 3M Force any single player to pay you 5M from their bank or properties.
It's My Birthday 2M All other players must each pay you 2M.
Pass Go 1M Draw 2 extra cards from the deck.
Double The Rent 1M Play this with a Rent card to double the total rent amount due from players!
Rent Cards 1M–3M Charge opponents rent based on the number of properties you own of that color.
House (+3M) & Hotel (+4M) 3M / 4M Add to completed sets to boost rent value. Hotel requires a House first. Cannot be placed on Railroads or Utilities.

💳 Crucial Payment Rules

  • Cards in Play Only: You can NEVER pay from your hand! Only cards already placed in your Bank or Properties on the table can be used.
  • No Change Given: If you owe 2M and only have a 5M bill or property, you do not get change. Overpayments are lost!
  • Paying with Properties: If you have no cash in your bank, you must pay with properties in play. Properties paid to an opponent go straight to their properties area.
  • Bankrupt?: If you have no money and no properties in play, you pay nothing. Debts are not carried over to future turns!
